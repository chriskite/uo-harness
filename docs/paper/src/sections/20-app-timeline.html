Project timeline

The ten days from the first research prompt on 2026-09-27 to the 389th commit on 2026-10-06, the last before the 12:00 CDT snapshot, one entry per milestone, wrong turn or decision, grouped by day.

Times are local (CDT, UTC−5), the zone of every commit. Most entries carry the commit that landed the change as a source pointer. Entries without one come from the omp session logs (a user message, a live event the agent reported) or from the memory store. Filled dots mark the milestones the rest of the paper keeps returning to. The list merges three research datasets: the git-derived milestone list, the early-session timeline (09-27 → 10-01), and the late-session timeline (10-02 → 10-05), plus the commits, docs/NOTES.md and store junctures from 10-05 20:30 to the snapshot. Where they disagree on a time, we used the commit time. Live in-game events use the time the session log or the store recorded them.

A few patterns are easier to see here than in the chapters. Each day has one dominant theme: the cipher on 09-27/28, movement and the decode correction on 09-29, the overseer and the audit on 09-30, safety on 10-02/03, home logistics on 10-04/05, and on the morning of 10-06 fixes for what the lumber shifts ran into. No commit landed between 01:00 and 08:59, and 31% (119 of 389) landed at 20:00 or later git log ee59524. Wrong turns are usually corrected within the same day.
